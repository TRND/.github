# Contributing to TRND repositories

## Access and eligibility

Contributions to TRND repositories are limited to authorized team members. Access to source code, development environments, and non-public work items requires authorization. If you need access for assigned work, use your established private TRND contact.

## Preparing a change

1. Confirm the repository, scope, and acceptance criteria for your assigned work. Follow the repository’s own guidance for requirements specific to that project.
2. Keep the change focused. Explain the intended outcome and identify affected behavior, dependencies, or integrations when relevant.
3. Verify the change. Record the checks you ran, their results, and any remaining validation gaps. Use redacted evidence and synthetic examples where possible.
4. Submit the change through the repository’s review workflow. Follow its branch protections, approval requirements, and release controls. Describe any operational impact and recovery steps when applicable.
5. Keep credentials, customer information, non-public links, and unredacted logs out of GitHub posts. Share sensitive evidence only through an agreed private channel.

## Where to get help

Use the [support guidance](https://github.com/TRND/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) for help with authorized work. Report vulnerabilities privately by following the [security policy](https://github.com/TRND/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
