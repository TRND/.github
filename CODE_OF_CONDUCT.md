# Code of conduct

## Scope

These standards apply to authorized participation in TRND repositories and their associated collaboration spaces. Follow applicable workplace and project policies alongside this guidance.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect. Keep feedback constructive and focused on the work.
- Make collaboration accessible and inclusive. Respect personal boundaries and address disagreement professionally.
- Maintain an environment free of harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and threats.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality. Handle code, work items, and personal information within their authorized channels.

## Reporting a concern

Raise concerns privately through your established TRND contact or a repository maintainer. If the concern involves that person, use another authorized TRND contact. If you need help finding the appropriate reporting route, use the contact on the [TRND GitHub profile](https://github.com/TRND) to request a private channel. Share only the context needed to understand the concern, and keep identifying details and evidence out of public GitHub posts.

Authorized maintainers handle reports under the applicable organization policies.
