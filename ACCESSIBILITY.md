# Accessibility

TRND aims to make its products, documentation, and collaboration materials usable by people with a range of access needs. Authorized contributors should consider keyboard navigation, assistive technology, readable content, and alternatives for time-based or visual material when planning and reviewing changes.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Use your established private support channel. If you need help finding it, follow the [support guidance](https://github.com/TRND/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).

Describe the product or material, what you were trying to do, and where you encountered a barrier. If useful, include the device, browser, or assistive technology you were using and a format or workaround that would help. Share only information you are authorized to disclose. Keep personal information and sensitive evidence in the agreed private channel.
